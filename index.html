1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Chess Clock handles data. Our application provides timing controls for chess matches and board games while respecting user privacy.

2. Introduction

Smart Chess Clock is designed to function locally on your mobile device. You can use all chess timing controls, presets, and customization options without creating an account or providing personal information.

3. Information We Collect

Smart Chess Clock does not directly collect personal information from users.

The app does not collect, record, or request any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts
  • Location data (GPS, IP location, or network location)
  • Photos, videos, files, camera, or microphone access
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, IDFA, or advertising IDs)
  • User account or login credentials

4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Smart Chess Clock stores settings and customization options locally on your physical device using platform-local storage (Android SharedPreferences). This information remains stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers or any remote database.

Category Locally Stored Data
1. App Settings Theme mode, appearance mode (dark/bright), player tile colors, timer text color, sound profile, sound volume, switch sound toggle, low-time warning toggle, out-of-time alert toggle, haptic feedback setting, millisecond display mode, timer orientation, icon size, timer text size, move counter toggle, screen keep-awake toggle, and auto-pause settings.
2. Custom Presets Preset name/title entered by the user, preset category, base time, delay/increment settings (Fischer, Bronstein, Simple Delay, USCF Delay), multi-stage configurations, and a locally generated random preset identifier (UUID) created for local indexing.
3. Selected Preset The ID of the currently selected preset.

5. Network Requests and Google Fonts

All chess clock features and game timing operations run locally on your device.

The application uses the google_fonts package for typography. Font files may be downloaded over HTTPS from Google's font CDN (fonts.gstatic.com).

Clarification of Network Communication

Smart Chess Clock does not directly collect personal information. Separately, when font files are downloaded over HTTPS, Google infrastructure may receive standard internet communication metadata, such as the connecting IP address.

Smart Chess Clock itself does not collect, store, analyze, or use your IP address.

6. Permissions Used by the App

The release application requests the following Android system permissions for specific functional features:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE
    Purpose: Used for haptic feedback when the app's haptic functionality is enabled.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK
    Purpose: Used to keep the device screen awake while the clock is being used when the relevant setting is enabled.

The app does NOT request or use permissions for: Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Bluetooth, Storage access, or Notifications.

7. Third-Party Services

Smart Chess Clock does not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, cloud databases, cloud storage, tracking SDKs, or third-party user data APIs. Font asset delivery via google_fonts is described in Section 5.

8. Advertising and Tracking

  • No Advertisements: The app does not display ads or use advertising SDKs such as AdMob.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app contains no tracking SDKs or analytics frameworks.
  • No Cookies: The app does not use cookies or web tracking.

9. Data Sharing

Smart Chess Clock does not collect personal information and does not send user preferences or custom presets to our servers. Consequently, no user data is shared with or sold to third parties.

10. Data Security

Settings, preferences, and custom presets are stored locally on your device within the application's sandboxed platform storage. No game settings or presets are uploaded to external databases.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Settings and custom presets remain stored locally on your device. You can remove this locally stored information by:

  1. Deleting custom presets within the app, or
  2. Clearing the app's data through your device's Android settings (Settings > Apps > Smart Chess Clock > Storage > Clear Data), or
  3. Uninstalling the app from your device.

Because there is no server-side user database, remote data deletion requests cannot be processed as no user data is stored on remote servers.

12. Children's Privacy

Smart Chess Clock does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The app operates without user accounts or personal data collection.
